FAQ
Questions families ask

Does DaNA decide if we should marry?

No. DaNA explains your options. The decision is always yours, and every choice is respected.

Will my family know I sent the alert?

No. Sila alerts are anonymous and never mention you, the condition or your family.

What if my result is low risk?

Good news! We explain what the test can and can’t tell you, and how you can still protect relatives.

Can I talk to a real person?

Yes. Licensed genetic counsellors are available, and urgent cases are contacted first.
